SideInstaller won't install? Other ways

iPhone only (experimental): the official iSH bootstrap signs SideInstaller using your own Apple Account instead of a shared certificate. It requires the App Store iSH app, terminal setup, a local certificate trust step and a lengthy first build. The developer has not verified a complete installation on an iPhone. Only use and review the original source before entering credentials.

Windows (more established): use official iPASide with your own free Apple Account, an iPhone cable and Apple Devices/iTunes. Download the verified Cinemana IPA below, drag it into iPASide, sign in and install, then enable auto-refresh. This does not use public certificates. Cinemana still needs real-device testing.

Neither route removes Apple's signing rules. Free-account installs generally expire after seven days. Do not install random signing profiles or enter your Apple password on this site.

Updates & help

Keep the app working

With a free Apple Account, signatures normally expire after 7 days. Before expiry, reconnect LocalDevVPN and re-sign/reinstall Cinemana in your installer. Enable available expiry reminders; do not assume background refresh succeeds. Refresh the installer itself if it uses your free account. Keep the same account and bundle ID to preserve data; back up first. If expired, you may need to reinstall the installer before recovering Cinemana.

Unable to verify or install?

A revoked public certificate cannot be fixed by this page. Do not keep adding unknown profiles or disable Apple security checks. Check the official installer’s status or use a computer once. For pairing errors, confirm Developer Mode, local VPN and the pairing type required by your iOS version. Free accounts also have app and App ID limits; extensions consume additional IDs.

Can I install without a signing tool?

The simplest supported future route is a reviewed TestFlight invitation: free for testers, paid Developer Program for the publisher, and builds expire after 90 days. Registered-device Ad Hoc distribution also requires paid membership and device registration, with limited device slots and expiring profiles. Neither route is currently available here. Public distribution requires appropriate content rights and Apple review compliance.

Protect your account

This website never asks for Apple credentials, certificates or pairing files. Only use official installer builds and review their terms. Installers contact Apple and may use third-party authentication helpers; upstream privacy claims are not an independent security audit. Remove unneeded DNS profiles in Settings after reviewing the provider’s instructions.
