Privacy policy

This page describes the current independent Cinemana iOS prototype. It is not a promise about third-party streaming providers or future features. A contact email and public privacy URL must be finalized before distribution.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your local watchlist, video viewing progress, download records, preferences and downloaded media in its application storage. The current app has no developer-run user account or cross-device syncing service.

Network requests and third parties

Catalog, search, streaming, subtitle and download requests are made directly to the configured third-party media provider. That provider and the network service may receive connection information such as your IP address and requested media URLs and process it according to their own policies. We do not control third-party retention practices.

Analytics and advertising

The current source does not include a developer-operated advertising SDK or analytics collection service. Apple's distribution, TestFlight, and diagnostic features may collect their own information under Apple's policies. If analytics or other services are added, this policy must be updated before release.

Deletion and retention

Watch history, watchlist, cache and offline media can be managed in the app. Uninstalling the application removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. The app cannot delete information that a third-party provider independently processed from your network requests.

Permissions

The player uses iOS audio and video playback facilities. Downloads require storage space; Wi-Fi-only downloads can be enabled. The app does not need a developer-operated account to browse the prototype.

Children

Media catalogs may include mature titles, and suitability varies by provider. The app does not currently provide a dedicated children's profile or parental-controls system.

Contact and policy changes

Maintainer profile: GitHub — Subaru771. A dedicated support/contact email will be required before Apple public submission. Material changes to data collection or processing will be reflected in a revised policy.

Release notice: This app and website are in distribution preparation. No public TestFlight invite or App Store listing is available yet. Streaming and offline downloading require appropriate provider authorization.
