# Cinemana 2.1.1 scene-skip refinements

- iOS: player settings > My scene markers > Mark start, then Mark end after the scene. The local range persists by episode/video ID.
- iOS and TV: Undo Skip button is available for about 18 seconds after a skip. The restored mature scene is exempt from auto-skip for the remaining playback session (TV) or already marked as skipped (iOS).
- No device-side AI scanning. No claim of content safety where markers are missing.
- This is a source beta, with Xcode/Gradle builds needed before device QA.
